PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana – Rooftop Solar Subsidy

MNRE

SARKARI ALERT

Important Details & Eligibility

EligibilityResidential consumers with a grid-connected local DISCOM connection, subject to scheme conditions.
Typical central subsidy₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, ₹78,000 for 3 kW or above under standard rates. Confirm local applicable rules.
ApplicationRegister on the national portal, select DISCOM and follow vendor and installation instructions.
Portal availabilityOfficial portal identified by PIB; live application page returned access error during review. No current submission guarantee.
